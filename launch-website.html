Questions buyers ask

Clear answers before you start.

If something's still unclear after this, reach out directly before you purchase.

What exactly is included in the $999 website?

A custom-designed, mobile-responsive business website of up to 5 standard pages (or an equivalent multi-section single page), a contact or quote-request form, an on-page SEO foundation, deployment, launch QA, and one revision round.

How many pages do I get?

Up to 5 standard pages, or one multi-section page covering the same ground — whichever fits your business better.

How long does it take?

Typical launch window: 7–14 business days after all required content and assets are received. The project clock starts once Wavy Sites has what it needs from you, not on the day you pay.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, photos, services or menu, business information, and any examples you like. The build starts once these are in hand.

How does payment work?

$999 total. A $499 deposit reserves your project today; the remaining $500 is due before final launch. No hidden fees and no preselected add-ons.

Can I request changes?

Yes — one primary revision round is included after your first draft. Larger or open-ended change requests are handled as a custom add-on.

What if I need an online store?

Ecommerce isn't part of the $999 package. It's a great fit for a custom project — choose Online store / ecommerce in this page's scope check to get a custom estimate, or use the main contact form.

What if I need a CRM or customer portal?

Custom software, CRMs, and customer portals are scoped separately as a custom project — Wavy Sites builds these too, just not inside the fixed $999 package.

Do I need hosting or a domain already?

If you already have a domain, we'll connect it at launch. If not, we'll walk you through getting one during onboarding.

Is SEO included?

Yes — page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, alt text, sitemap, and robots file are included as a baseline foundation.

What happens after I pay?

You'll get a short intake step to send your business details and assets. The build begins once we have what we need.

Is the payment refundable?

The $499 project-start deposit is non-refundable once work begins because it reserves production time and covers work performed. See the Website Launch Package Terms for cancellation, scope, and timeline details.

Do you provide maintenance afterward?

Yes. Ongoing maintenance, support, and improvement plans are available after launch.
