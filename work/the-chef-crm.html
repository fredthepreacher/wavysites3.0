Software case study · Hospitality & catering

The Chef Catering CRM.

A full operational system behind a catering business's public site — turning every inquiry into a tracked booking, quoted, scheduled, and paid, without manual re-entry.

Start Your Project ← Back to Work

The problem

A menu and a contact form aren't a booking system.

A catering business runs on more than a nice-looking site. Every inquiry needs to become a tracked lead, get quoted against a real menu, get scheduled against a venue, get insurance and readiness checked before the event, and get paid — deposit and balance — without the details living across email threads, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. That was the gap: a beautiful public-facing menu with no operational system behind it.

What Wavy Sites built

  • A full CRM behind the public site: leads, clients, events, and venues in one place
  • A quote-to-calendar workflow, from first inquiry to a scheduled, staffed event
  • Stripe-powered deposit and balance payments, tied to each booking
  • Certificate-of-insurance and event-readiness tracking before every booking
  • An internal AI assistant to speed up day-to-day operations — with hard guardrails

What's inside

Built in phases, each one shipped and verified.

The system was built and accepted in stages — intake and client management first, then venue and insurance tracking, then the quote-to-payment workflow.

01

Leads, clients & events

Every public inquiry lands directly in the CRM as a tracked lead, with a dedicated record for each client and event — no manual copy-paste from an inbox.

02

Venues & readiness

Venue details, versioned menus, and a readiness check tied to each event — including certificate-of-insurance status — so nothing gets booked half-prepared.

03

Quotes, calendar & revenue

Quotes move straight to a shared calendar once approved, with a revenue view tying every booking back to what was actually quoted and paid.

04

Stripe deposits & balances

Clients pay deposits and balances directly against their booking through Stripe — no separate invoicing tool, no chasing payment status by hand.

The AI assistant

Built to help — never to decide.

The CRM includes an internal AI assistant to speed up day-to-day work. It runs under an explicit guardrail written directly into the system: it cannot change or invent prices, discounts, contract terms, payment status, refunds, quote approval, booking status, menu approval, or certificate-of-insurance approval. Every output is reviewed by the operator before it's used — the assistant speeds up the work, it doesn't make the calls.

Business capability demonstrated

This is the difference between a website and a system: a business that used to track bookings across email and spreadsheets now runs leads, venues, quotes, insurance readiness, and payment collection from one connected tool — with an AI assistant that accelerates the work without being trusted to make judgment calls it shouldn't make.
