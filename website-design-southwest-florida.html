Can Wavy Sites repair a site it didn't originally make?

Often, yes. We first check your platform, access and the size of the issue. A repair request costs nothing to submit.

Do I need to meet in person?

Not for most website work. We can review a public URL, discuss changes remotely and provide a written scope.

No. We make pages easier for people and search engines to understand, but no agency can guarantee Google, Maps or AI-answer placement.
